SDG 6: Clean Water and Sanitation

Sustainable Development Goal 6 aims to ensure availability and sustainable management of water and sanitation for all by 2030.

🎯 Target 6.1

Achieve universal and equitable access to safe and affordable drinking water for all.

🎯 Target 6.2

Achieve access to adequate and equitable sanitation and hygiene for all, ending open defecation.

🎯 Target 6.3

Improve water quality by reducing pollution, eliminating dumping, and minimizing release of hazardous chemicals.

🎯 Target 6.4

Increase water-use efficiency and ensure sustainable withdrawals of freshwater resources.

Our Contribution

Aqua Impact directly contributes to achieving SDG 6 through community-based water projects, sanitation development, and education.

Water and Sanitation Issues

⚠️ Water Scarcity

Over 2 billion people worldwide live in countries experiencing high water stress.

⚠️ Contaminated Water Sources

Unsafe water causes diseases like cholera, dysentery, and typhoid.

⚠️ Lack of Sanitation Facilities

Around 3.6 billion people lack access to safely managed sanitation services.

⚠️ Poor Hygiene Practices

Inadequate handwashing and hygiene education contribute to the spread of diseases.

⚠️ Inequitable Access

Rural communities, women, and marginalized populations face disproportionate challenges in accessing clean water.

Prevention and Solutions

✅ Water Conservation

Implement rainwater harvesting systems, fix leaks promptly, and use water-efficient appliances.

✅ Water Treatment Systems

Install filtration and purification systems to ensure safe drinking water.

✅ Proper Sanitation Infrastructure

Build and maintain toilets, sewage systems, and waste treatment plants.

✅ Hygiene Education

Promote handwashing with soap, proper sanitation practices, and awareness about waterborne diseases.

✅ Protect Water Sources

Prevent pollution and protect watersheds and aquifers.

✅ Community Engagement

Involve local communities in water management decisions.
